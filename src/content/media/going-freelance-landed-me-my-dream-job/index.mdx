Fee Mak knew from an early age that a career in the media industry was what she wanted to do. Over the past 10 years, she has built a successful freelance media career, navigating the highs and lows of working for herself and continually finding ways to get her foot in the door.

She now regularly presents her own show every week on a national radio station, with thousands of people tuning in each week.

This is Fee’s story – and how she did it.

Converge is zeroheight’s annual design systems conference. In 2026, we hosted the event at the Glasshouse, Newcastle, on 22nd October

SPEAKER: 
Daniel Yuschick
Lead Design System Developer - OP Financial Group 

TALK: From Codebase to Community: Rebuilding Trust in Legacy Design Systems.
What happens when a design system builds in isolation, lacks documentation, and ships constant breaking changes? You don't just get technical debt, you get trust debt. Users stop contributing, start "doing it themselves," and the system becomes a legacy burden. In this practical case study of two of Finland's largest institutions, we go behind the scenes to show how we modernized not just their codebases, but their communities. We'll explore the transition from a "black box" model to a transparent platform rooted in automated workflows, inclusive governance, and meaningful feedback loops. We will discuss the reality of realigning organizational structures and incentive models to shift the culture from gatekeeping to true community contribution. Join us to learn how to move beyond shipping components and start building toward a sustainable culture and systemic trust.

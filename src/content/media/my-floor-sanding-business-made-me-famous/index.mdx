Growing up on a council estate and living with ADHD, Danny Stenhouse never imagined he’d end up where he is today.

He had many different jobs in his twenties and thirties before eventually finding his calling: running a floor sanding business and posting flooring transformation videos that are watched by millions of people.

This is Danny’s story – and how he did it.

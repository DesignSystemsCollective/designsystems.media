Converge is zeroheight’s annual design systems conference. In 2026, we hosted the event at the Glasshouse, Newcastle, on 22nd October

SPEAKER: 
Lauren LoPrete - Head of Design Foundations, Mercury

TALK: Design Systems Anarchy 
Design systems got good at control, which in my opinion was never the job. This talk draws a line between punk culture and design systems - three principles, one argument: the teams that build conditions for creative chaos will outlast the ones trying to govern it. With AI making the means of production more accessible than ever, the gatekeeping model more than wrong, I think it's kinda obsolete.

Come find out what it looks like to build a scene instead.

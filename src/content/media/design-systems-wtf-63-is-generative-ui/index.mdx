In this episode of Design Systems WTF, Luke Murphy and Michelle Chin dive deep into the world of Generative UI, Declarative UI, and Probabilistic UX. They explore whether we’re heading toward a world where we stop designing fixed interfaces and start designing the "guardrails" that allow AI to build them in real-time.

In this episode, Luke and Michelle discuss:

- What is Generative UI? Defining the shift from fixed screens to AI-assembled components
- The Death of the Browser: Could hyper-personalised, conversational interfaces replace the apps and websites we use today?
- Seeding Control: Why the hardest part of AI design isn't the technology - it’s the "uncomfortable" shift for designers to let go of the pixels
- Design Systems as the Backbone: Why a robust design system is the only way to make Generative UI work reliably

Whether you're curious about AI in design systems, looking to optimise your design tokens, or wondering how systems thinking will evolve in an AI world, this episode covers it all.

Your teams now ship with AI agents, and every agent has to know how your product should look and work. That knowledge lives in your design system, spread across Figma, Storybook, code and docs and kept in sync by hand, so agents guess and teams drift.

In this under-3-minute walkthrough, we show you how Supernova, the agentic design system platform, turns your design system into one source of truth for your teams and their AI agents. It does four jobs: manage your design data, document it, deliver it to code and to agents, and improve it from how it's used.

You'll see how to:
✦ Connect Figma, Storybook, Tokens Studio and your codebase into one design system
✦ Keep every token and component in sync across themes and brands
✦ Build documentation from live data, and let the Supernova Agent write the pages for review
✦ Turn a token change into pull requests for web, iOS and Android
✦ Give Cursor, Claude Code and Copilot your design system through per-team MCP servers
✦ See how the system is used, and fix the gaps agents report

Design systems used to be a niche topic. Now that AI agents build so much of our UI, they've become a crucial part of how product teams work, and our booth at WaysConf 2026 in Kraków was busy for two days straight.

In this 2-minute look-back, we share what we heard from the designers and design system teams who stopped by, including Supernova customers who run their design system on it every day.

You'll hear:
✦ Why design systems matter more now that AI agents are everywhere
✦ How Supernova brings together data from Figma, Storybook and code, and turns it into documentation for your teams and their agents
✦ Why teams use Supernova as their single source of truth
✦ How Supernova works as a bridge between design and development
✦ How a UX writer keeps rules, guidelines and a glossary in Supernova, and connects it to Claude through the Supernova MCP
✦ Why keeping big teams aligned is still the hardest part

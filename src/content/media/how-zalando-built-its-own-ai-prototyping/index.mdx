Figma Make didn't give the Zalando Design System team on-brand prototypes. So they built their own AI prototyping tool with Claude Code. It turns a Figma design or a prompt into a working Zalando prototype with real product data.

Dana Baddad (Senior Product Designer at Zalando) and Gonzalo Vásquez Correa (Senior Product Designer, AI enabler and builder on the Design Systems ZDS team at Zalando) show the ZDS Capsule live at the Into Design Systems meetup at Zalando in Berlin. Gonzalo runs a Claude Code skill that starts agents in parallel and uses the Figma MCP to rebuild a Figma design as a prototype. Dana shows what happened when they rolled it out to the designers at Zalando and why the biggest failure came before the first workshop started.

You will learn:
→ The 5 foundations that make a design system AI-ready
→ Why you should split guidelines into many small design.md files with an index
→ How a Claude Code skill builds a prototype from a Figma design with the Figma MCP
→ Why skills should run scripts at the critical step
→ What an engineer said about AI-generated motion specs

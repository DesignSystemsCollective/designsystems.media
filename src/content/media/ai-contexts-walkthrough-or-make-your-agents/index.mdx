Supernova AI context helps you curate and govern AI contexts, centralizing and distributing design system and engineering knowledge via MCP to all agents in your company.

You can filter knowledge and data in each context so you provide your teams with what their agents actually need, which makes them sharper, faster and more efficient than if you give them everything at once.

Your brand blue shows up in 400 places in your product, and now it has to change. Design tokens are how that becomes one change instead of 400.

A design token is a design decision with a name. Change its value and everything that uses the token follows. Colors are only the start: spacing, corner radius, fonts and shadows work the same way. A good rule of thumb is that if you keep writing the same value, it should probably be a token.

Name tokens for their purpose. Call it primary rather than blue, because in a year or two your primary color might not be blue anymore.

In Supernova, all your tokens live in one place and work with your themes, so dark mode just switches the values, even across several brands. When a token changes, your developers get a pull request and your documentation updates too.

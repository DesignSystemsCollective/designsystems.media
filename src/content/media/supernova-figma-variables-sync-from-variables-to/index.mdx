Your design decisions live as variables in Figma, but your design system needs them as tokens, with every mode and brand intact. Copying them over by hand is slow, and they drift the moment someone changes a color.

In this under-3-minute walkthrough, we show you how to bring your Figma variables into Supernova with the Figma Variables Sync plugin. You choose a design system, select your collections, set up modes as themes, push, and push again whenever a variable changes.

You'll see how to:
✦ Find Supernova Figma Variables Sync in the Figma Community and sign in with your Supernova account
✦ Choose the workspace and design system your tokens go to
✦ Select your collections, with referenced ones like Base brought along automatically
✦ Set the base token value and bring every mode in as a theme
✦ Click Push collections to Supernova and find your tokens in Design Tokens
✦ Click Push updates to Supernova whenever a variable changes in Figma
✦ Map each brand to its own dataset, with extended collections or brands as modes

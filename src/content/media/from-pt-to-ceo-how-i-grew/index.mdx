Lewis Pratten spent years building his experience in the fitness industry as a personal trainer and rugby player. But after coaching an entrepreneur, he saw an opportunity to take his passion for fitness further.

Together, they built Lewis’s dream business: Hyre, a Bristol-based gym centred on group exercise and community.

There was just one problem: they launched the gym just as COVID-19 was around the corner.

This is Lewis’s story – and how he did it.

You launched your design system and everybody clapped. A week later the same question is back in Slack. A design system is never finished, and it gets better through the feedback you collect.

People and AI agents run into gaps while they work, and each gap can turn into a fix. Collect feedback right where people already work, so they don't have to go somewhere else to give it.

When someone works around the system, treat it as a missing piece in your documentation or your data. The workaround shows you what the system doesn't cover yet.

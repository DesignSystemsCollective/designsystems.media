Converge is zeroheight’s annual design systems conference. In 2026, we hosted the event at the Glasshouse, Newcastle, on 22nd October

SPEAKER: 
Dan Donald, Design systems consultant

TALK: Navigating Friction

We don't know if AI is good for design systems. It's probably both. That's not a new feeling for us; navigating trade-offs is the job. This is a talk about the friction of working with AI, the ethics and instability we can't control, and where the real opportunity lies for teams sitting at the centre of every organisation.

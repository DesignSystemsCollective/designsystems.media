Converge is zeroheight’s annual design systems conference. In 2026, we hosted the event at the Glasshouse, Newcastle, on 22nd October

SPEAKER: 
TJ Pitre - Product Architect, AI and Design Systems - Figma


TALK: AI Without the Chaos: Context-Based Design Systems in Practice

AI is fast, and it's exceptionally good at exposing the cracks in how your team designs and builds products. Without structure, it amplifies confusion rather than clarity. In this session, TJ shares recent, real-world experience with AI and design systems in production: how teams use context to guide AI, which approaches fall apart (and why), and where a structured design system begins to do real work across design and development.

He'll introduce a Context-Based Design Systems model for moving past prompt experimentation toward patterns you can actually repeat, with concrete examples and live demos along the way. You'll leave with a clearer mental model for working with AI in product teams, and a better sense of where your design system is pulling its weight.

Converge is zeroheight’s annual design systems conference. In 2026, we hosted the event at the Glasshouse, Newcastle, on 22nd October

SPEAKER: 
Nikki Plyem
Design Manager, Product Platform - DocPlanner

TALK: Patterns Over Parts: What Makes Your System Unique, and Who Holds the Answers.
For years, design system teams operated like a UI policing force - shipping components and enforcing visual consistency across feature teams. But in an era where UI elements are commoditized and AI can generate components in seconds, a component library is no longer your competitive advantage. What truly makes a product unique aren't the parts, but the opinionated patterns that bind them together

You open your vibe-coded app and find fourteen buttons, and no two of them look the same. That's the problem components solve.

A component is a piece of UI you build once and reuse across your product, instead of rebuilding it again and again. It comes in a few variants, like primary and secondary, has properties you can change, like size and state, and under the hood it's built from your tokens. If you're making something more than once, it should probably be a component.

Name variants for their purpose. A button is primary or secondary, never blue or purple, so the name still holds when the color changes.

Lucy Bowes is the founder of Lucy & Lola, a pet fashion business turning customers’ favourite four-legged companions into bespoke designs. Since launching in 2021, the business has generated more than £2 million in turnover.

What began as a side hustle run from a spare room has since grown into a thriving company with an expanding team, earning Lucy a place on Forbes’ 30 Under 30 list 2026.

This is Lucy’s story – and how she did it.

Building design tokens without a plan can cause more harm than good. Philipp Jeroma shows how to plan a design token system before you build it: with five pillars of planning, a design token starter canvas in FigJam and a starter set in Figma that turns the plan into variables.

This is the recording of Philipp Jeroma's session at the Into Design Systems Conference 2025. Philipp (Design Lead / Global HMI Coordinator at STULZ Digital Solutions, ex Europace AG) walks through a fictional cyberpunk corporation whose product director demands design tokens now. He explains why a Figma or CSS variable is not a design token but the outcome of one. Then he answers the planning questions live in his canvas and builds the first token version in Figma.

You will learn:
→ Why design tokens and variables are not the same thing and when variables are enough
→ The five pillars of planning: strategic alignment, organization and governance, architecture and scaling, infrastructure and maintenance
→ How to define an MVP for your tokens, turn values into decisions and name them so your users understand them
→ When to export variables with a plugin and when to automate with Tokens Studio
→ How library metrics in Figma showed 20k button instances before a breaking change
